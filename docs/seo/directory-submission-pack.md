# Directory submission pack — copy-paste listing data

> **Purpose:** one place to copy from when submitting Ballena & Beluga to directories,
> tourism boards, OTAs, and citation sites. Built from `src/lib/contact.ts` and
> `src/lib/data.ts` — the same single source of truth the site's JSON-LD uses, so a
> listing made from this pack can't drift from the site's schema. **Drift is the enemy:**
> Google cross-references your listed Name/Address/Phone (NAP) against the site's
> structured data when deciding whether all these profiles are *one* business. Keep
> every field below byte-for-byte identical across every listing.

target list), [`growth-plan-2026-06.md`](growth-plan-2026-06.md) (Pillar 2 = this work).

**★ Authority note:** items 1–3 (the tourism boards + DZS) are the real authority wins —
they're high-DA, topically perfect, and let you list `www.ballenaandbeluga.com` directly.
Do those three even if you skip everything else this week. The aggregators (9–10) give
booking *distribution* but weak SEO value: they usually link to the booking funnel (often
`nofollow`) rather than your site, and they're one referring domain regardless. Don't spend
the bulk of your two hours there.

**Track results** in the spreadsheet schema at the bottom of

## 5 — Consistency rules (protect the NAP)

1. **Same website host everywhere:** `https://www.ballenaandbeluga.com` (with `www`).
2. **Same phone format everywhere:** `+385 91 2524 094`.
3. **Location = Svetvinčenat**, never Zagreb (Zagreb only in legal/billing fields).
4. **Keep the diacritic:** Svetvinčenat (with č). Don't substitute "Svetvincenat" in a
   *location* field if the form accepts UTF-8 — but it's fine in a URL slug, which is ASCII by design.
5. **One Airbnb-fed listing per aggregator** — don't also create a manual duplicate (HomeToGo/
   Holidu pulling your Airbnb + a second hand-made entry = duplicate, which Google dislikes).
6. After a listing goes live, **add its public URL to the villa's `listingUrls` in
   `src/lib/contact.ts`** so the site's JSON-LD `sameAs` cites it back — that two-way link is
   what consolidates the brand entity. (Claude can wire this once you send the live URLs.)
