### How to pitch

Don't blast the same email everywhere. For each target:

1. Find the actual journalist who covers Croatia / villa rentals / interior design (LinkedIn search "Travel Editor [Outlet]" or "Croatia correspondent").
2. Read their last 3 articles. Reference one in your email subject line.
3. Pitch a *story*, not a product. Stories that work for villa rentals: "the rise of inland Istria as a luxury alternative to Dubrovnik", "what 'biological pool' actually means", "how Croatian wedding venues changed after EU accession", "designer-built rentals as alternative to hotels for 2026".

Email template — adapt per outlet:

```
Subject: Story idea: [SPECIFIC ANGLE that ties to their beat]

Hi [Name],

I read your [recent article on X] and noticed you cover [their beat] for [outlet].

I run two designer villas in Svetvinčenat in central Istria — built 2021,
4 en-suite bedrooms each, biological pool, sauna. They're popular with the
"slow-travel inland Istria" crowd that has grown ~3× since 2022.

Story angle that may fit your readers: [SPECIFIC ANGLE]. Happy to share a
photo set, hosting facts, or arrange a press visit.

Photos: https://www.ballenaandbeluga.com/en/gallery
Press kit (PDF): [HOSTED ON GOOGLE DRIVE — TODO: assemble]

Worth a 15-minute call?

Best,
Marijan Pojatina
+385 91 2524 094
```

## Tier 3 — Community & user-generated content

Lower authority per link but cumulatively meaningful, and unlocks the DataEase **Reddit visibility** axis (0/15 today).

### Reddit posts (~3–5 quality posts over 60 days)

- `r/Croatia` (260k subs) — post a high-quality interior photo set with caption "Built two designer villas in central Istria — happy to share what we learned about Croatian villa-rental regulations" (sharing knowledge, not sales). Avoid pure self-promotion or it gets removed.
- `r/travel` (10M subs) — only post in the regular "trip report" or "where to stay" megathread; never standalone unless something visually exceptional.
- `r/solotravel` (3M subs) — pitch the wellness angle (Finnish sauna + biological pool).
- `r/AskCroatia` — answer 10–20 genuine questions over a few weeks; build account karma before mentioning the villas.
- `r/digitalnomad` — slow-season (October–April) wellness retreats angle.

### Other communities

- TripAdvisor Croatia forum — answer 20+ questions about Istria over 3 months, signature with villa URL.
- Lonely Planet Thorn Tree (Croatia) — same playbook.
- Croatia Facebook groups: *Croatia Travel Tips*, *Istria Lovers*, *Wedding Venues Croatia*.
- Two Croatian YouTube travel vloggers: pitch a sponsored stay (in-kind: 2 nights free in shoulder season).

## Tier 4 — Partnerships & cross-links

Free backlinks from natural partners.

- Local wineries (Kozlović, Clai, Coronica, Kabola) — offer to feature them on `/experiences` with a link in exchange for a "where to stay" page link from theirs.
- Local restaurants (Konoba Morgan, Pizzeria Grimani, Stari Tisak) — same swap.
- Truffle hunting outfits (Karlić Tartufi, Zigante) — same swap.
- Tour operators in Pula (PulaTours, Istra ON) — affiliate-style: they reference us as luxury overnight, we send transfer business their way.
- Wedding planners in Istria (Lavanda Weddings, Fivelements) — vendor cross-link.

## Tier 5 — HARO / Help A Reporter Out

Sign up at https://www.helpareporter.com (US) and https://www.qwoted.com — both deliver journalist queries 1–3× per day. Filter for `Croatia`, `Istria`, `villa`, `vacation rental`, `wedding venue`, `digital nomad`, `wellness retreat`. A successful pitch typically results in a quote in a national outlet with a backlink — DA 50–90 per landing.

Plan for 3 pitches per week × 12 weeks = 36 pitches → realistic conversion 3–6 published mentions.
