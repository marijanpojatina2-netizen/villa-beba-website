# FeWo-direkt listing plan — Villa Ballena & Beluga

A step-by-step playbook for listing the villas on **FeWo-direkt**, the dominant
German vacation-rental portal. Owner action — not a code task. This file is the
reference; work through it top to bottom.

## Why FeWo-direkt

- It is *the* portal Germans use to search for a **Ferienhaus**. Germany is the
  #1 source market for Istria (22.3M overnight stays in Croatia in 2025).
- Part of the **Vrbo / Expedia Group** network — one listing automatically
  appears on FeWo-direkt **and** Vrbo **and** the wider Expedia network.
- **Free to list.** You pay only on a booking: **8% total** = 5% commission +
  3% payment processing. Much cheaper than Airbnb / Booking.com (~15%).

## Strategy decisions

**Two listings, one per villa: Villa Ballena and Villa Beluga.**
FeWo-direkt lists one rental unit at a time. Do *not* try to create a third
"Complex BeBa" listing — the portal cannot handle "book both villas together".
Instead, in each listing's description, add one line that the neighbouring
villa can be combined for groups up to 18 (see drafted copy below). Large-group
inquiries then come to you **direct via ballenaandbeluga.com**, where there is
no 8% fee.

**Fee model:** commission-based is the only option for new listings (the old
annual subscription is discontinued). 8% is fine — accept it.

## ⚠️ Do this FIRST: calendar sync

Once live on FeWo-direkt you will have **three** booking sources: Airbnb,
FeWo-direkt/Vrbo, and direct website bookings. Three unsynced calendars = a
double-booking sooner or later. Before publishing the FeWo listings, set up
calendar sync — either:
- **iCal links** exchanged between Airbnb, FeWo-direkt and your direct calendar
  (free, manual, ~hourly refresh — adequate for low volume), or
- a **channel manager** (Smoobu, Lodgify, Hostaway — ~€20–50/month) that keeps
  all calendars in one place (recommended once you have steady bookings).

This is non-negotiable. Set it up before the listings go live.

## Step-by-step

1. Go to **fewo-direkt.de → "Unterkunft inserieren"** (List your property).
2. Enter bedrooms (4), bathrooms, full address; verify the pin on the map;
   create a host account.
3. Fill the listing — title, description, amenities, photos, pricing,
   availability calendar, house rules, cancellation policy (see below).
4. Repeat the whole process for the second villa.
5. Connect the calendars (see warning above).
6. Publish both listings.

### Photos

25–40 photos per villa. Lead with the pool and the exterior (the first photo is
the one that wins the click). Include every bedroom, all bathrooms, the kitchen,
the sauna / game room, the terrace, the tennis court, and a few of the village
and surroundings. Landscape orientation, high resolution, no text or logo
overlays.

## How this feeds the website (the SEO point)

This is not only a booking channel — it is a discovery and authority surface:

- **Use the exact villa names** ("Villa Ballena", "Villa Beluga") and the exact
  location ("Svetvinčenat, Istrien") — identical to the website. Germans who
  find the villa on FeWo-direkt then Google the name; consistent naming makes
  that funnel land on **ballenaandbeluga.com** and strengthens the brand entity
  Google builds.
- The listing is a **brand citation** on a high-authority domain — it helps the
  young domain's trust signal.
- **Reviews** collected on FeWo-direkt/Vrbo compound trust over time.
- Do **not** write "book cheaper direct" in the listing — platforms forbid it.
  Brand-name consistency does the job legitimately: researching and repeat
  guests find the direct site on their own.

## After going live

- Ask **every** guest (FeWo, Airbnb and direct) for a review — reviews are the
  single biggest trust lever for a young listing and a young domain alike.
- Keep pricing and the calendar in sync across all channels.

## Next German channels (after FeWo-direkt is running)

- **HolidayCheck** — the big German travel-review platform. Create a property
  profile and direct guests there for reviews; strong trust signal for Germans.
- **Atraveo / Interhome** — German agency portals for Croatia villas; optional,
  higher commission, consider only if you want more reach.
